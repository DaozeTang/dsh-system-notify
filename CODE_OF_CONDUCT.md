# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a useful, low-drama place to collaborate. Be
respectful, assume good intent, and critique ideas rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the plugin and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who do
not follow this code. Report concerns through the repository's private
maintainer contact or a GitHub report. Reports are handled confidentially.

This is a lightweight code of conduct for a small plugin, not a legal
document.
